---
title: The deck
description: The deck is today's short list of what to do next, and why, made fresh each morning from your tasks, deadlines and calendar.
---

You can only hold a few things in your head. The deck picks them for you: a short, ranked stack of what to do today, with the reason for each, so you start the day knowing where your attention goes instead of rereading your whole list.

The deck is on your Home, beside your main chat. On a phone it's the **Deck** tab.

## What's on it

- **Deadlines** sit at the top whenever something is due soon or overdue, so a real date never hides in the stack.
- **Today** is the stack itself: a handful of tasks in the order to do them. Open a card's reasoning to see why it's there and why now. Drag cards to reorder them.
- **More options** has alternatives, for when the top of the stack doesn't fit the moment.

Above the stack, filter by energy (**Light** or **Deep**) and by area, so the deck fits the time and focus you have.

## How it's made

Ri makes a new deck the first time you open it each day, and refreshes it each morning before you start. It reads your tasks, deadlines and calendar, and what you've told it in **Deck sources**.

**Deck sources** is in **Settings**, under **Profile**. Tell your deck, in plain words, how to plan your day and which connected services to use, like your calendar or a task tracker. The deck reads it every time it refreshes.

To make a fresh deck now, use **Generate New Deck** at the top. Earlier versions of the day's deck stay under **Versions**. Turn the morning refresh on or off at the top of the deck, or in **Settings**, under **General**.

You can also ask your [main chat](/docs/concepts/main-chat): "move the tax return to the top", "what should I do next?", "plan my afternoon around the dentist".

## Keep it in view

On a wide screen, the **Deck** button in the top bar shows the deck beside whatever you're doing, a chat or an agent. Search (⌘K or Ctrl+K) has **Show deck beside** too.

The deck is part of your own Ri. A team has no deck.
