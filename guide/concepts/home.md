Your Home is your Ri: your tasks, notes, agents and chats, and the server that runs your agents. It lives on one computer, in one folder you own. Because everything is in one place, your agents and you always work from the same list, and nothing lives only in your head or in one app.

## One computer runs it

The computer you chose with **Start a new Ri** (or `ri start`) is your Home's computer. It keeps your data and runs your agents. Your phone, a browser and your other computers [connect to it](/docs/getting-started/devices). They show the same Ri, always in sync, and keep nothing of their own.

That's why the computer matters. If it sleeps, your agents pause. A computer that stays on, like a Mac mini or a server, keeps them working while you're away. See [Keep Ri running on a computer that stays on](/docs/guides/always-on).

You can move your Home to another computer later without losing anything. See [Your data and backups](/docs/guides/data-and-backups).

## Where it lives

| How you installed Ri | Your Home's folder |
| --- | --- |
| On a server, with `ri start` | `~/ri` |
| The desktop app on a Mac | `~/Library/Application Support/Ri/home` |
| The desktop app on Windows | `%APPDATA%\Ri\home` |
| The desktop app on Linux | `~/.config/Ri/home` |

If the desktop app found an existing Ri in `~/ri` and you chose **Use existing Ri on this computer**, that's your Home. **Settings**, under **This computer**, shows the folder your Ri is using.

## What's in it

Your Home holds your tasks, notes and chats, the files you attach, and a few files you can read and edit yourself, like `USER.md` and `SOUL.md`, which tell your assistant about you. [Your data and backups](/docs/guides/data-and-backups) lists what's there and what to keep safe.

## Your Ri and your teams

Your own Home is called **My Ri**. A team has a Home of its own, on a computer of its own, with shared tasks and notes. Nothing from My Ri is copied into a team, and your private planning stays yours. The deck, the scratchpad and your assistant's personal files belong to My Ri only. See [Teams](/docs/concepts/teams).
