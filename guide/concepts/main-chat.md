Your main chat is your assistant. It keeps your tasks, notes, deck and stream in order, and it can see and steer every agent's work. When you don't know where something is, or which agent is doing what, ask it.

It's the **Chat** tab on your Home, beside the deck. On a phone it's the **Chat** tab at the bottom.

## What it does for you

- **Tasks and notes.** "Remind me to renew my passport in March." "What did I decide about the pricing page?" It searches before it creates anything, so you don't get duplicates.
- **Your day.** "What's on my plate today?" It works from your [deck](/docs/concepts/deck) and your deadlines.
- **Your agents.** "What needs me across my agents?" It reads what each chat is doing, nudges one that stalled, starts a new chat with a good brief, and answers a chat's questions when your intent is clear.
- **The Stream.** It sorts what you [capture](/docs/concepts/capture-and-stream) into tasks and notes, and every choice can be undone.
- **Questions about Ri.** It reads these docs, so "how do I connect my phone?" gets an answer that matches the version you run.

It works through Ri itself, the same way the app does, and never edits the files in your Home behind Ri's back. There are things it leaves to you: only you approve a permission a chat asks for, and it archives a chat only when you ask.

## Its name and face

Your assistant is called Ri until you name it. In **Settings**, under **Profile**, **Your assistant** sets its name and face: an emoji on a color, a picture, or art. The same page has **What should your assistant call you?** and **About you**, a few lines about you that your agents can read.

## What it knows about you

Three files in your [Home's folder](/docs/concepts/home) shape how your assistant works with you:

| File | What it's for | Who edits it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `USER.md` | Who you are and how you like to work: your projects, your hours, how much detail you want, what's off limits. | You. Ri reads it and never changes it. |
| `SOUL.md` | Your assistant's voice: its tone, its judgment, and what it should never do. | You. Ri reads it and never changes it. |
| `MEMORY.md` | What your assistant learned and decided across conversations. | Your assistant keeps it current, and you can edit it too. |

Open them in any text editor. A few honest lines in `USER.md` save you from explaining yourself in every chat.

## Starting fresh

The main chat runs for days. When you want a clean start, click **New** at the top of the chat. It archives the current one, and **History** beside it keeps your past main chats.

Each [agent](/docs/concepts/agents) has a main chat of its own too, which coordinates that agent's work.
