---
title: Scratchpad
description: One page of working memory beside every screen, for what you're holding right now.
---

Some things you only need for the next hour: the three steps you're juggling, a number to remember, the message you'll send an agent when it finishes. The scratchpad is a single page for exactly that. It stays in view beside whatever you're doing, and it's the same page on every device.

Nothing reads it, sorts it or acts on it on its own. It's yours to jot on, reorder, cross things off and clear.

## Open it

Press ⌘⇧J (Ctrl+Shift+J on Windows and Linux), or click **Scratchpad** in the top bar. On a wide screen it opens as a column beside the page. **Expand** gives it more room.

## Move a line out

When a line turns into something to keep, click it (or select some text) and use the buttons at the bottom of the pad:

- **Task** or **Note** makes it a [task or note](/docs/concepts/tasks-and-notes) and leaves a link behind on the pad.
- **Stream** hands it to the [Stream](/docs/concepts/capture-and-stream) for your assistant to sort, and clears it from the pad.
- **Chat** adds it to the message you're writing.

## Agents and the scratchpad

Agents don't read your scratchpad unless you point them at it, and they can't change it. Ask your main chat to "look at my scratchpad" when you want its help with what's there.

Each chat also has a scratchpad of its own, in its side panel's More menu. It's handy for drafting your next message while an agent works.

The scratchpad is part of your own Ri. A team has no scratchpad.
