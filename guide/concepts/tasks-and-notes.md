title: Tasks and notes
description: One list that you and your agents share, with notes beside it, so nothing lives only in your head.
---

Your tasks and notes are the same list for you and your agents. You add a task from your phone, an agent picks it up, and your main chat sees both. Because agents do the upkeep, the list stays true without a weekly review.

Something to do is a task. Something to keep, like an idea, a link or a reference, is a note.

## Tasks

A task moves through a few states:

| State | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Consider** | A possibility, not a commitment yet. Good for "maybe someday". |
| **Todo** | Something you've committed to. New tasks start here. |
| **In progress** | Being worked on, by you or an agent. |
| **Done** | Finished. |
| **Archived** | Set aside. **Restore** brings it back to Todo. |

A task can also carry:

- **Deadline**, for a real date it must be done by. Deadlines are pinned to the top of your [deck](/docs/concepts/deck).
- **Energy**, **Deep** or **Light**, and **Effort**, from **XS: Trivial** to **XL: Epic**, so you can match work to the time and focus you have.
- **Priority**, and **Snooze until** to hide it until a date.
- **Repeat**, typed the way you'd say it, like "every 2 weeks" or "3d". When you complete a repeating task, it rolls to its next date, shown as **Next**.
- **Blocked on**, for what it's waiting for, with **Unblock** when it's clear.

Ask your main chat to make tasks for you ("add a task to call the dentist next Tuesday"). It writes a clear title and picks sensible details.

## Hand a task to an agent

Open a task and click **Start with agent**. Ri opens a new chat with the task as its brief. Pick the agent and click **Start**, and Ri links the chat to the task and moves the task to In progress. The chat shows the task at its top. On a task already in progress, **Continue with agent** opens the chat working on it.

## Notes

Notes hold everything that isn't a task: meeting notes, research, ideas, links. A note can also be a **Decision**: what was decided and why, so you and your agents don't argue it again later. The notes list has a **Decisions** filter.

## Areas

Areas group your tasks, notes and agents by part of your life, like Work, Home or Health, so you can look at one at a time. Find them on your Home under **More**, in **Areas**.

## Links between things

Type `@` in a task or note to link another task or note. The link shows as a chip you can click, and the other page lists it under **Linked references**. Agents use the same links, so a chat's summary can point straight at the task it finished.

You can add images and files to a task or note too.

## Archive and delete

Agents never delete anything. They archive, and archiving can be undone. You can archive or delete a task or note from its **More actions** menu. Delete is permanent, so archive when in doubt.

## Search

Search (⌘K or Ctrl+K) finds any task, note, decision or capture from a few of its words, so you don't have to remember where you put something. The filters across the top narrow it to one kind: **All**, **Tasks**, **Notes**, **Decisions**, **Stream**, **Apps** once you have one, and **Commands**. Press Tab to move to the next filter, or click one. Typing `task:` or `>` picks Tasks or Commands too.

In a team, search has **Chats** in place of **Stream**.

## Other ways to see your work

- **Board**, in the rail: your tasks in columns by state, or what your agents are doing.
- **Calendar**, in the rail: your week or day, with your agents' work beside your events.
- **Tasks** and **Notes**, on your Home: the full lists, with filters.

When you join a Google Meet from Calendar, Ri asks Google to use the email of the connected calendar account. Sign in to that Google account in the browser that opens the link. Google may still ask you to choose an account or sign in.
