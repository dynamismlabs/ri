---
title: Your first run
description: What Ri asks the first time it opens, and why each answer helps.
---

There's no setup wizard. The first time your Ri opens, your [main chat](/docs/concepts/main-chat) asks a few short questions, one at a time, and skips anything it already knows. Every answer can change later, and you can stop with **Skip setup** once a harness is ready.

## 1. Name your assistant

"Hi, welcome to Ri." Your main chat is your assistant, and the first question is what it should go by. Pick a name and a look: an emoji on a color, a picture, or art. It's called Ri until you choose. Change it any time in **Settings**, under **Profile**, in **Your assistant**.

## 2. Pick a harness

Ri needs an AI tool to think with. That's the harness, like Claude Code or Codex, and it runs on the plan you already pay for.

If Ri finds Codex or Claude Code already signed in on your computer, it uses it and tells you which model it set as your default, with a way to change it. Otherwise it shows you the harnesses to pick from. Pick one, install it and sign in if you haven't, and Ri checks that it works by sending it one short request. [Harnesses](/docs/guides/harnesses) has the install and sign-in steps for each.

In the desktop app, Claude Code and Codex install and sign in with one click.

## 3. Tell it about you

Ri asks what to call you. If you've used Claude Code, Codex or OpenCode on this computer, it offers to bring in your recent projects and chats, so your agents start with your history. It then drafts a line about what you're working on for you to confirm or fix.

## 4. Areas, accounts and a first agent

- **Areas** group your tasks, notes and agents, so you can look at one part of your life at a time. Ri suggests some from what you've told it. Pick any that fit.
- **Your accounts.** "Want me to work in the apps you already use?" Connect Gmail, Google Calendar, Slack and others, and your agents can read and act in them. Ri checks with you before it sends anything or does anything that can't be undone. See [Connectors](/docs/concepts/connectors).
- **A first agent.** Your agents work in folders on your computer, like a code repo or a folder of documents. Add one and your main chat can start work there. See [Your first agent and chat](/docs/getting-started/first-agent).

## After setup

With nothing in Ri yet, your main chat asks what you need to get done and starts you on **Capture your first task**. Finish the sentence in your own words and send it. Your assistant saves it as your first task and links it in its reply, so one click opens it. **Plan my day with me** and **What can you do for me?** sit beside it if you'd rather start there.

Under them is **Use Ri on your phone**, until you pair one. Your phone needs a secure address for Ri, one that starts with https. It lets you talk to Ri with the microphone and reach it away from home.

- **With a secure address,** the pairing form opens right there. Click **Create pairing link** and scan the QR code with your phone. Ri tells you once your phone is in.
- **Without one,** **Set up phone access** opens the remote address in **Settings**, under **Devices**. Save it there and choose **Back to pairing**. The pairing form is waiting in your main chat.

[Your phone and other devices](/docs/getting-started/devices) has the details.

Ri has no setup checklist. The rest is extra, and your assistant mentions each one when it would help: notifications, the agent browser, your accounts, and Ri inside your other AI tools.
