Ri should feel calm to sit in all day, so how it looks is yours to set. Both choices below belong to the browser or app you set them in. Your phone and your computer can look different, and nothing changes for anyone else on your Ri.

## Light or dark

In **Settings**, open **General** and turn **Dark mode** on or off.

## Try the new look

Ri has a new design on the way. Turn on **New look (preview)** in **Settings**, under **General**, to use it now:

- Calmer grays, with the sidebar and top bar one step softer than the work beside them.
- Your computer's own font, a little tighter, so more fits on screen.
- Labels in normal sentence case instead of small capitals.
- The tabs at the top of each panel, and every other either-or choice, in one rounded switch.
- Task and note details as plain words, and deadlines as a colored date instead of a colored box.
- Rounded controls on a task, like the choice of energy and effort, instead of small boxes.
- A white message box that stands out from the page.
- Matching controls in chats, Calendar, Settings and scheduled tasks, with a clear outline when you move by keyboard.
- The same controls when choosing an agent, connecting an account or recovering work. File chips stay readable inside your own messages.

The preview keeps your layouts and your light or dark setting. Turn it off at any time to go straight back.
