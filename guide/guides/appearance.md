Ri should feel calm to sit in all day, so how it looks is yours to set. Your choice belongs to the browser or app you set it in. Your phone and your computer can look different, and nothing changes for anyone else on your Ri.

## Light or dark

In **Settings**, open **General** and turn **Dark mode** on or off.

## Ri's design

Every screen stays where you know it. What changed is how it's drawn, so the whole app feels calm and consistent:

- Calmer grays, with the sidebar and top bar one step softer than the work beside them.
- Your computer's own font, a little tighter, so more fits on screen.
- Labels in normal sentence case instead of small capitals.
- Every either-or choice, like the tabs at the top of each panel, in one rounded switch.
- Task and note details as plain words, and deadlines as a colored date instead of a colored box.
- Rounded buttons in consistent sizes, with larger controls on a phone and a clear outline when you move by keyboard.
- A white message box that stands out from the page.

Hover over an icon button, or move to it with the keyboard, to see what it does.

Use the arrow keys, Home or End to move along a rounded switch. Tabs open their panel as you move. Filters such as Unread and Working apply the focused choice when you press Space or Enter. On a task, press the selected Energy or Effort again to clear it.

Search, Settings and Schedules share the same quiet backdrop.

On a phone, filters have more room to tap. The message controls stay together. The model shows its name, with its settings in the model menu. On a narrow screen, the mode uses its icon. Run summaries sit above their smaller details, and the full calendar wraps its controls so you can reach them all.

The desktop app's setup and recovery screens use the same colors and rounded controls as the rest of Ri. They use your computer's font too, including while Ri is starting or finding text on a page.
