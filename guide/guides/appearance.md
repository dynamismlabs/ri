title: Appearance
description: Choose automatic, light or dark for the browser or app you use Ri in.
---

Ri should feel calm to sit in all day, so how it looks is yours to set. Your choice belongs to the browser or app you set it in. Your phone and your computer can look different, and nothing changes for anyone else on your Ri.

## Automatic, light or dark

In **Settings**, open **General** and pick a **Theme**:

- **Automatic** matches your computer or phone. When it switches between light and dark, at sunset for example, Ri switches too. Ri starts on Automatic.
- **Light** and **Dark** stay the same whatever your device does.

On a phone, the same choice is in **More**. You can also search Ri (⌘K or Ctrl+K) for "theme" to switch without opening Settings.

## Button shape

Buttons, switches and small labels are fully round by default. To try softer square corners instead, open **General** in **Settings** and choose **Rounded** under **Button shape**. Choose **Round** to go back. Like the theme, the choice belongs to the browser or app you set it in.

## Ri's design

Every screen stays where you know it. What changed is how it's drawn, so the whole app feels calm and consistent:

- Calmer grays, with the sidebar and top bar one step softer than the work beside them.
- Your computer's own font, a little tighter, so more fits on screen.
- Labels in normal sentence case instead of small capitals.
- Every either-or choice, like the tabs at the top of each panel, in one rounded switch.
- Task and note details as plain words, and deadlines as a colored date instead of a colored box.
- Rounded buttons in consistent sizes, with larger controls on a phone and a clear outline when you move by keyboard.
- Cards, panels and fields are white, or black in dark mode, with a thin border. Gray marks the things you press and what's selected. Status labels keep their meaning in both themes.
- Text fields keep their labels, help and errors together, with more room to type on a phone.
- A white message box that stands out from the page.

The top bar and the bottom of the sidebar stay quiet: their buttons and counts sit plainly until you point at them, and a count that needs you keeps its color.

Hover over an icon button, or move to it with the keyboard, to see what it does.

Use the arrow keys, Home or End to move along a rounded switch. Tabs open their panel as you move. Filters such as Unread and Working apply the focused choice when you press Space or Enter. On a task, press the selected Energy or Effort again to clear it.

In a narrow file header, More actions holds the actions for revealing the file or opening it in your editor or an app.

You can read your search and reach Clear even in a narrow Files column. The count and shortcut appear when there is room.

Search, Settings and Schedules share one backdrop. It dims the screen behind them, so the window in front stands out.

On a phone, filters have more room to tap. The message controls stay together. The model shows its name, with its settings in the model menu. On a narrow screen, the mode uses its icon. Run summaries sit above their smaller details, and the full calendar wraps its controls so you can reach them all.

The desktop app's setup and recovery screens use the same colors and rounded controls as the rest of Ri. They use your computer's font too, including while Ri is starting or finding text on a page.

On a phone, a form keeps its main action visible while you scroll its fields. Field labels and choice titles use the same readable type.

Dialogs, menus and previews share the same quiet frame. Press Escape to close the open menu or popup, then Escape again to close the dialog beneath it. Keyboard focus returns to the control you opened it from.
