title: Notifications
description: Get told the moment an agent needs you, on your computer, your phone or Telegram, and nothing more.
---

The point of handing work to agents is to stop watching them. Notifications tell you when one needs you, so you can look away until then.

## Turn them on

In **Settings**, open **Notifications**. It tells you in one line whether you're set up and what to fix.

- **Your computer:** the desktop app shows alerts when an agent needs you or finishes a chat. Turn them on under **Notifications** in the desktop app, and allow Ri in your computer's notification settings when it asks.
- **Your phone and browsers:** open Ri on your phone and turn on browser push there. A phone needs your Ri's HTTPS address for push, and on an iPhone, Ri must be added to the home screen first. See [Your phone and other devices](/docs/getting-started/devices).
- **Telegram:** **Connect Telegram** sends alerts to a Telegram chat.

Each channel can send a test, and you can turn one off or remove it.

## Choose what reaches you

The routing table sets which events go to which channel:

- An agent needs your input.
- A chat finished.
- An approval is needed.
- The deck surfaced something.
- A message from your main chat.
- A scheduled run's result.
- The disk is almost full.

Under **Advanced**, scheduled digests send you a summary on a schedule instead of one alert at a time.

On a Mac, the desktop app's notifications need a copy of Ri signed by Apple. Preview builds can't show them.
